# 19. UX states & polish

**Priority:** Quality · **Theme:** Quality · **Checks:** 21

The small states and touches that make an app feel finished instead of generated.

- [ ] **Make a custom 404 page**: and a friendly 500 page, both with a way back.
- [ ] **Put a CTA above the fold**: visitors should see what to do without scrolling.
- [ ] **Add a sticky CTA on mobile**: the button stays in reach as people scroll.
- [ ] **Show loading states**: spinners or skeletons, so nothing looks frozen.
- [ ] **Show form error states**: inline, specific and human-readable.
- [ ] **Show form success states**: plus a thank-you page that says what happens next.
- [ ] **Confirm destructive actions**: a modal before delete, cancel or anything you can't undo.
- [ ] **Add hover states**: on everything clickable, so it's obvious what's interactive.
- [ ] **Make mobile menus work**: open, close, scroll and link correctly on a real phone.
- [ ] **Add a dark mode toggle**: default to the system setting via `prefers-color-scheme`.
- [ ] **Add site search**: once you have more than a handful of pages.
- [ ] **Add a back-to-top button**: on long pages.
- [ ] **Use a sticky header**: navigation stays reachable on long pages.
- [ ] **Add a scroll progress bar**: on long articles and docs.
- [ ] **Add copy buttons**: on codes, links and snippets.
- [ ] **Add a password visibility toggle**: fewer typos, fewer resets.
- [ ] **Use an expandable FAQ**: answers on demand without a wall of text.
- [ ] **Add a floating contact button or chat**: help is one tap away.
- [ ] **Show a "last updated" date**: on docs, policies and articles.
- [ ] **Add a print stylesheet**: for receipts, invoices and docs people actually print.
- [ ] **Keep the cookie banner simple**: clear accept and reject buttons (the legal rules are in section 18).
