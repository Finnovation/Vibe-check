# 21. Accessibility

**Priority:** Quality · **Theme:** Quality · **Checks:** 5

Usable by everyone, including keyboard and screen reader users. A legal requirement in some places.

- [ ] **Write useful alt text**: describe meaningful images, and use empty `alt=""` on decorative ones.
- [ ] **Meet WCAG AA colour contrast**: 4.5:1 for body text.
- [ ] **Support keyboard navigation**: everything reachable and usable with Tab, Enter and Esc, with visible focus rings.
- [ ] **Add a "skip to content" link**: keyboard users shouldn't tab through your whole nav on every page.
- [ ] **Label every form field**: and make sure errors are announced to screen readers.
