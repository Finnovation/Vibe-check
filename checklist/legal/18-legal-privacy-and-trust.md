# 18. Legal, privacy & trust

**Priority:** Launch · **Theme:** Legal · **Checks:** 15

Not legal advice. Requirements differ by country (GDPR, CCPA, the Australian Privacy Act and more), so check what applies to you.

- [ ] **Publish a privacy policy**: what you collect, why, who you share it with, and how to get it deleted.
- [ ] **Publish terms of service**: the rules for using your product and the limits of your liability.
- [ ] **Publish a refund policy**: payment providers and consumer law expect one.
- [ ] **Add a cookie policy and a real consent banner**: non-essential cookies stay off until the user agrees (GDPR/ePrivacy).
- [ ] **Check form consents**: explicit, unticked opt-ins for marketing.
- [ ] **Collect only the data you need**: every extra field is a liability in a breach.
- [ ] **Handle data deletion requests**: have a process, and actually delete.
- [ ] **Put an unsubscribe link in every marketing email**: it's the law in most places.
- [ ] **Get age consent for kids' data**: COPPA, GDPR-K and local equivalents.
- [ ] **Remove dark patterns**: no trick cancellations, pre-ticked boxes or confirm-shaming.
- [ ] **Remove fake reviews and testimonials**: illegal in many countries, and trust dies when they're spotted.
- [ ] **Remove unsupported claims**: "#1", "bank-grade", "used by 10,000+". If you can't prove it, cut it.
- [ ] **Add business details**: legal name, ABN/company number where required.
- [ ] **Show a real contact address**: required for businesses in many countries, and it builds trust.
- [ ] **License fonts and images**: AI-grabbed assets are often not yours to use.
