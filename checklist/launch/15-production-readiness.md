# 15. Production readiness

**Priority:** Launch · **Theme:** Launch · **Checks:** 7

The difference between a demo and a product people trust.

- [ ] **Kill localhost URLs**: grep for `localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, ngrok and staging URLs.
- [ ] **Remove lorem ipsum**: and every other scrap of placeholder content.
- [ ] **Send email from your own domain**: with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up, or your mail lands in spam.
- [ ] **Use a real support email**: one that someone actually reads.
- [ ] **Track signups and activation**: know whether people get value, not just whether they arrive.
- [ ] **Install analytics**: respecting the consent rules in section 18.
- [ ] **Use UTM tracking on campaigns**: so you know which channels actually work.
